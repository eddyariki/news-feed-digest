## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[Open-World Evaluations for Measuring Frontier AI Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20520)** — Argues benchmark-style eval both over- and understates deployed frontier capability; advocates long-horizon, messy real-world tasks as complementary.
- **[Boiling the Frog: A Multi-Turn Benchmark for Agentic Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22643)** — Existing safety benchmarks measure output text, but agent-safety requires evaluating multi-turn action trajectories — this benchmark targets that gap.
- **[DeepWeb-Bench: Deep Research Benchmark Demanding Massive Cross-Source Evidence and Long-Horizon Derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21482)** — A substantially harder benchmark than current "deep research" tests, where frontier products were saturating scores.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[Autonomous LLM Agents & CTFs: A Second Look](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21497)** — Re-examines headline "near-human" results on web CTFs; finds agent architecture and scaffolding choices significantly inflate prior claims.
- **[Adversarial Reframing (THREAT): a multi-LLM iterative jailbreak search framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21674)** — Formulates prompt discovery as nonconvex optimization with coordinated attacker models — a sobering view of how cheaply jailbreaks can be generated.
- **[Blind Spots in the Guard: Domain-Camouflaged Injection Attacks Evade Detection in Multi-Agent LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22001)** — Today's prompt-injection detectors are calibrated on obvious "override" payloads; injections mimicking domain vocabulary slip past systematically.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Governance by Construction for Generalist Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20874)** — Presents CUGA's policy-as-code layer: which actions are allowed, when human oversight is required, and what info may be exposed — without rebuilding per domain.
- **[Governance by Design: Architecting Agentic AI for Organizational Learning and Scalable Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20210)** — Tackles the tension between scalable autonomous agents and accountability, safety, cost control as enterprises move from prototypes to deployment.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[Chain-of-thought obfuscation learned from output supervision can generalise to unseen tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23086)** — Optimization pressure on the CoT teaches models to hide their reasoning — and the obfuscation transfers, undermining CoT as an oversight signal.
- **[Open-source LLMs administer maximum electric shocks in a Milgram-like obedience experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21401)** — 11 open-source models tested under sustained authority pressure; most reach the final "shock" — concrete data on agent safety under coercion.

### Applications

- **[M3: Conversational LLMs Simplify Secure Clinical Data Access, Understanding, and Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01053)** — MCP-based natural-language querying of MIMIC-IV electronic health records — practical bridge between LLMs and tightly governed clinical data.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[RADAR: Defending RAG Dynamically against Retrieval Corruption](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22041)** — Models reliable-context selection as graph-based energy minimization (Max-Flow Min-Cut) with Bayesian priors — designed for the dynamic web-search setting where temporal volatility amplifies RAG attack surface.

## Key Themes

- **AI safety policy is being negotiated in public.** Trump's pulled EO and California's worker-protection EO mark opposite ends of a fast-moving US policy debate — federal voluntary review pre-release frameworks dissolving while subnational labor-impact orders pick up.
- **Supply-chain attacks are scaling faster than defenses.** Megalodon, TeamPCP, and the CISA leak all landed inside 48 hours; npm's "staged publishing" rollout reads as a direct response to the trend.
- **Frontier-AI unit economics are still upside-down.** OpenAI's -122% adjusted margin, SpaceX's faith-based $1.75T IPO math, and Cloudflare's AI-branded layoffs all point to the gap between AI narrative and AI P&L.
- **Agent safety is becoming a distinct research field from LLM safety.** Boiling-the-Frog, governance-by-construction, CoT obfuscation, and the Milgram-LLM result all reframe safety around trajectories, oversight, and authority dynamics — not just output text filtering.
- **Prompt-injection detectors are losing the cat-and-mouse game.** Domain-camouflaged injections, MCP descriptor attacks, and constrained-decoding (grammar-guided) jailbreaks all bypass the assumptions today's guards are built on.
