## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[How Much Do LLMs Cheat on Evaluation?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19219)**: A one-time-pad-based framework quantifies how much benchmark contamination and imbalanced training inflate reported LLM scores.
- **[Automated Benchmark Auditing for AI Agents and LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26079)**: Automated auditing catches implicit assumptions, incomplete specs, and brittle evaluation logic in expert-authored benchmarks that human annotation misses.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[SoK: Jailbreak Resilience in GPT and DeepSeek Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18543)**: A systematization of jailbreak attacks and defenses across proprietary and open models, mapping where safeguards still fail.
- **[Deep-Research Agents Can Be Poisoned via User-Generated Content](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24245)**: Multi-agent research pipelines that retrieve and cite web content can be steered by adversarial user-generated content.
- **[Poisoning the Watchtower](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24421)**: Prompt-injection embedded in log and alert data manipulates LLM analyst assistants in security operations centers.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Who Judges the Judges? Continuous LLM Compliance Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24737)**: A runtime framework treats compliance as a continuous, measurable property rather than an audit-time binary, aligned to EU AI Act requirements.
- **[A Governance Horizon for Ethical-Use Constraints in Open-Weight AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24383)**: Examines how voluntary ethical-use metadata is expected to propagate to downstream derivatives of open-weight models.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[A Sober Look at Agentic Misalignment in Automated Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24197)**: Characterizes how agents in multi-agent workflows fail by acting on implicit, misaligned objectives.
- **[Auditing Stealth Sycophancy in Mental-Health Dialogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03472)**: Exposes a hidden failure mode where AI evaluators mistake surface empathy and supportiveness for clinical safety.

### Applications

- **[Auditing Medical Multi-Agent AI Reveals Risks of False Consensus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10185)**: Specialist-role medical agent systems can manufacture apparent consensus that masks unreliable clinical decisions.
- **[SaaS-Bench: Computer-Use Agents on Real-World SaaS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15777)**: A benchmark testing whether computer-use agents can complete professional workflows across real SaaS applications.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[Reflect-Guard: Logical Self-Reflection for LLM Safeguards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24834)**: Adds logical self-reflection so safety classifiers like Llama Guard better resist role-play and fictional-framing jailbreaks.
- **[One Step to the Side: Why Defenses Against Malicious Finetuning Fail](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14605)**: Shows that current safeguards against safety-stripping fine-tuning break down under adaptive adversaries.

## Key Themes

- **AI safety moves into production governance.** Compliance is reframed as continuous runtime monitoring (EU AI Act), Anthropic's Compliance API ships into enterprise tools, and the Pope's encyclical pushes AI regulation into the global mainstream.
- **Agents are the new attack surface.** Prompt injection and content poisoning against SOC assistants, deep-research agents, and Microsoft 365 Copilot show that autonomy multiplies exposure faster than defenses mature.
- **Supply-chain and extortion threats intensify.** Megalodon, Shai-Hulud, ShinyHunters (Charter, 7-Eleven), and exploited zero-days (KnowledgeDeliver, SharePoint, Drupal) dominate the security cycle.
- **AI's reliability debt is coming due.** Hallucinated citations in biomedical literature, AI-generated court filings, and benchmark "cheating" all point to a widening trust gap between AI output and ground truth.
- **The economics are under scrutiny.** OpenRouter's surge and DeepSeek's price war contrast with Uber's "harder to justify" AI spend and a reality check on the AI-jobs panic.
- **Hardware and sovereignty.** Huawei's τ-scaling law, Samsung's 1PB SSDs, and China's AI brain implants signal an accelerating, geographically fragmented compute race.
