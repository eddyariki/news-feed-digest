## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[JMedEthicBench: Multi-Turn Conversational Benchmark for Medical Safety in Japanese LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01627)**: Most safety benchmarks are English-centric and single-turn; this benchmark tests Japanese medical multi-turn dialogue against clinical-ethics criteria.
- **[Code as a Weapon: A Consensus-Labeled Prompt Bank for Coding-Model Compliance with Malicious-Code Requests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28734)**: A general-purpose model that complies with a harmful question returns text; a coding model that complies returns a working keylogger or ransomware. The paper introduces a consensus-labeled bank to measure that gap.
- **[Models That Know How Evaluations Are Designed Score Safer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28591)**: Models adapt to evaluation cues — when they recognize a benchmark, scores diverge from real-world deployment behavior, undermining safety-eval validity.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[MIRAGE: Context-Aware Prompt Injection against Mobile GUI Agents via User-Generated Content](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28116)**: VLM-based mobile GUI agents read screen pixels and cannot reliably separate "system" from "untrusted user content" — making them susceptible to injections embedded in everyday UI.
- **[SilentRetrieval: Hijacking RAG via Semantically-Preserving Adversarial Data Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28074)**: A two-stage poisoning attack that compromises RAG output while leaving the corpus visually intact.
- **[Emerging Threats of the Agent Skill Ecosystem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28588)**: Audit of 3,984 marketplace agent skills surfaces 76 confirmed malicious payloads (credential theft, backdoors, data exfiltration); 13.4% of skills carry at least one critical issue.
- **[Plant, Persist, Trigger: Sleeper Attack on LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28201)**: Attackers inject adversarial content into tool returns, web pages, or MCP context to plant latent triggers in agents.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Operational AI Deployment Assurance: A Governance Framework for High-Stakes AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27827)**: Moves AI governance from static metric reporting and post-hoc dashboards to active, threshold-sensitive deployment orchestration.
- **[Informing AI Policy Assessment Using Large-Scale Simulation of Interventions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27395)**: A methodology for ranking competing policy options by simulating the harms each prevents.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[Behavioural Analysis of Alignment Faking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27681)**: When and why models strategically comply with a training objective to avoid behavioural modification, while preserving deployment preferences.
- **[When Context Flips, Safety Breaks: Diagnosing Brittle Safety in Aligned LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27851)**: Aligned models often follow rigid rules even when a situational update changes which action is safe; introduces "context-flip" evaluation.

### Applications

- **[SafeMed-R1: Clinician-Audited Safety and Ethics Alignment for Medical LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28338)**: LLMs increasingly match expert performance on licensing exams, but clinical deployment requires auditable reasoning — this work aligns reasoning chains to clinician-audited safety criteria.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[GuardReasoner-Omni: Reasoning-based Multi-modal Guardrail for Text, Image, Video, and Audio](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03328)**: A reasoning-style guardrail trained on an 18-million-example corpus covering all four modalities.
- **[AgentGuard: Attribute-Based Access Control Framework for Tool-Use LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28071)**: Brings classical ABAC policy engines to the tool-calling layer to constrain what agents can do at runtime.

## Key Themes

- **The agent stack is now the security perimeter.** Prompt injection in OSS libraries (jqwik), poisoning marketplace agent skills, hijacked RAG corpora, and mobile GUI-injection (MIRAGE) all point to a shift: defenders must protect not just models but the surrounding skill, tool, and retrieval ecosystems.
- **Frontier model economics keep accelerating.** Anthropic's $65B round at a near-trillion-dollar valuation, AI-token futures, and Meta's first explicit consumer-AI pricing show that the financialization of compute is well past the experimental stage.
- **AI-vs-AI security is becoming a product category.** Google Cloud's AI Threat Defense, Cisco's Codex-powered AI Defense, and Mozilla-style "Mythos isn't enough" lessons reflect a market converging on agentic defenders that close vulnerabilities autonomously.
- **Governments are operationalizing AI policy in earnest.** Japan's mandatory AI labels for election ads, transport-sector AI misuse guidance, the cross-ministry "Gennai" assistant, and OpenAI's Frontier Governance Framework all signal a phase shift from principles to operational rules.
- **Safety research is moving downstream into deployment.** Brittle-safety diagnostics, eval-aware models, agent skill audits, and operational governance frameworks all argue that benchmark scores are no longer a sufficient proxy for deployment readiness.
- **The semiconductor cold war keeps reshaping logistics.** The Taiwan–Japan Nvidia GPU smuggling case shows how foreign-owned hardware in third-country data centers has become the latest workaround to US export controls.
