## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[VERA-MH: Validation of Ethical and Responsible AI in Mental Health](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13318)** — Clinically-validated chatbot safety benchmark focused on suicidal-ideation risk; the same benchmark cited in today's coverage of The Path scoring 95/100 versus 65 for consumer bots.
- **[CTFExplorer: Evaluating LLM Offensive Agents Through Multi-Target Web CTF Benchmarking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08023)** — A multi-target Web CTF benchmark for measuring how well LLM agents conduct end-to-end offensive operations.
- **[Refusal Evaluation in Coding LLMs and Code Agents: A Systematic Review of Thirteen Malicious-Code Prompt Corpora (2023–2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20351)** — Systematic review of malicious-code prompt corpora used to assess whether coding LLMs and agents refuse harmful tasks.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[LASH: Adaptive Semantic Hybridization for Black-Box Jailbreaking of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21362)** — Black-box jailbreak method combining semantic hybridization strategies to defeat frontier-model refusals.
- **[REFLECTOR: Internalizing Step-wise Reflection against Indirect Jailbreak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20654)** — Trains models to internalize step-wise reflection as a defense against indirect prompt-injection style jailbreaks.
- **[VIPER-MCP: Detecting and Exploiting Taint-Style Vulnerabilities in Model Context Protocol Servers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21392)** — Identifies a new class of taint-style flaws in MCP servers (the protocol used by Claude Desktop and many agent stacks) and demonstrates exploitation.
- **[Detecting Trojaned DNNs via Spectral Regression Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21146)** — Spectral-regression detector for trojaned neural-network weights, useful for supply-chain integrity of shared models.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Auditing Apple's DifferentialPrivacy.framework: Implementation Bugs, Misconfigurations, and Practical Risks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21378)** — Independent audit surfaces real-world DP bugs and misconfigurations in Apple's framework, with practical privacy-loss implications.
- **[Enabling Regulatory Multi-Agent Collaboration: Architecture, Challenges, and Solutions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09215)** — Reference architecture for multi-agent systems that must operate under overlapping regulatory regimes.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[Towards Context-Invariant Safety Alignment for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20994)** — Aligns refusal behavior so safety holds under context shifts that typically erode it (long contexts, role-play wrappers, multi-turn).
- **[Backchaining Loss of Control Mitigations from Mission-Specific Benchmarks in National Security](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21095)** — Working backward from concrete national-security mission benchmarks to derive loss-of-control mitigations rather than abstract risk taxonomies.
- **[EvalMORAAL: Interpretable Chain-of-Thought and LLM-as-Judge Evaluation for Moral Alignment in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05942)** — Interpretable CoT + LLM-judge protocol for evaluating moral alignment, addressing brittleness in single-score moral benchmarks.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[Geometry-Lite: Interpretable Safety Probing via Layer-Wise Margin Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20241)** — Studies the layer-wise geometry of safety probes to explain how prompt-level safety detectors actually separate safe/unsafe inputs and what survives benchmark shift.
- **[Conformal Selective Acting: Anytime-Valid Risk Control for RLVR-Trained LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20270)** — Anytime-valid risk certificate for online-updated RLVR specialist LLMs under per-deployment error budgets.
- **[Heartbeat-Bound Hierarchical Credentials: Cryptographic Revocation for AI Agent Swarms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20704)** — Heartbeat-bound credential scheme to revoke compromised AI agents in real-time across multi-agent deployments.

## Key Themes

- **AI is now a working tool for both offense and defense in cybersecurity.** Two separate items this cycle — Anthropic's Mythos helping crack an Apple M5 kernel, and US Cyber Command's push to run frontier models on classified networks — point to the same near-term inflection. OpenAI's planned GPT-5.5-Cyber distribution to the Japanese government fits the same pattern, as does the new MCP-server vulnerability work (VIPER-MCP).
- **The AI-infrastructure money cycle is now publicly priced.** The SpaceX S-1 makes the Anthropic/Colossus deal ($15B/year), xAI's losses ($6.36B), and additional turbine spending ($2.8B) part of the public record at the same moment OpenAI is reportedly about to file confidentially and Nvidia posts another record quarter — but with the first explicit growth slowdown.
- **US AI governance is being pulled in two directions.** The Trump administration shelves a pre-release AI security review order while taking $2B in equity across quantum-computing firms, and researchers studying online safety sue the same administration. The signal: heavy industrial-policy investment, light regulatory touch on frontier-model safety.
- **Safety research is converging on production realities.** Today's papers cluster around concretely deployable controls — context-invariant safety alignment, layer-wise probe geometry, anytime-valid risk control for RLVR models, revocable credentials for agent swarms, and DP framework audits — rather than purely conceptual alignment work.
- **Agentic AI is the product story across every vendor.** Microsoft (MagenticLite/Fara), Alibaba (Qwen3.7-Max with 35-hour autonomous runs), Google (Agentic Browsing audits, consumer agents at I/O), Anthropic (Code with Claude, Claude Cowork) — every major lab shipped agentic product surface this cycle.
