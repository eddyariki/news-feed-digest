## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[GT-HarmBench: Benchmarking AI Safety Risks Through the Lens of Game Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12316)**: 1,535 high-stakes scenarios built on game-theoretic structures (Prisoner's Dilemma, Stag Hunt, Chicken) to probe multi-agent safety risks like coordination failure and conflict that single-agent benchmarks miss.
- **[HalluScan: A Systematic Benchmark for Detecting and Mitigating Hallucinations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02443)**: A comprehensive framework spanning 72 configurations to evaluate hallucination detection and mitigation in instruction-following LLMs.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[PoisonForge: Task-Level Targeted Poisoning Benchmark for Instruction-Tuned LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23168)**: Parameterizes a realistic data-supply-chain threat where a few crafted instruction–response pairs make a model embed attacker-specified entities for a target task while behaving normally elsewhere.
- **[Same Model, Different Weakness: Jailbreak Attack Surface in Frontier MLLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23157)**: The first systematic cross-lingual, multimodal red-teaming study, showing jailbreak vulnerability shifts between English and Mexican Spanish across Claude Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5, Pixtral Large, and Qwen Omni.
- **[MemAudit: Post-hoc Auditing of Poisoned Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23723)**: Uses causal attribution and structural anomaly detection to find malicious records injected into an LLM agent's persistent memory through ordinary interaction.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Redrawing the AI Map: A Theory of Accountability Boundaries in Agentic Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23179)**: Argues that AI capabilities requiring evidence, review, or assignable responsibility keep integrated accountability boundaries even as their technical interfaces become modular.
- **[Ontological Knowledge Blocks: Executable Compliance for Trustworthy AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23297)**: Replaces documentation-centric, checklist-based audits with machine-executable compliance and profile-based validation for transparency, accountability, fairness, and traceability.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[Model Spec Midtraining: Improving How Alignment Training Generalizes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02087)**: Introduces a midtraining stage to fix shallow alignment that generalizes poorly when models are fine-tuned only on demonstrations of spec-aligned behavior.
- **[Evaluating Large Language Models in a Complex Hidden Role Game](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22826)**: Measures reasoning, persuasion, and deception in the social-deduction game *Secret Hitler* via new metrics like Role Identification Accuracy and Deception Retention Rate.

### Applications

- **[Are Frontier LLMs Ready for Cybersecurity?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23243)**: A dual-mode benchmark — white-box vulnerability detection across C/Java/Python plus black-box web-app testing with 118 ground-truth flaws — evaluates six frontier models including Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.4.
- **[Diagnosing Temporal Failure Modes in LLM-based Statutory Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23497)**: Identifies post-cutoff staleness and recency bias in legal-research LLMs, where models apply superseded statutes or wrongly prefer newer provisions.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[Test-Time Training Undermines Safety Guardrails](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22984)**: Shows that inference-time parameter adaptation opens three new threat models attackers can exploit to jailbreak otherwise-safe models.
- **[Prompt Overflow: What the Guardrail Inspects Is Not What the Model Infers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23196)**: Demonstrates that truncation- and segmentation-based handling of overlength prompts lets injection attacks slip past safety checkers into the underlying LLM.

## Key Themes

- **AI security goes mainstream and high-stakes.** Anthropic's Project Glasswing/Claude Mythos surfaced 10,000+ vulnerabilities, regulators (Japan's FSA and BOJ) issued formal preparedness directives, and new attacks — Noroboto's "lying fonts," chatbot-personality exploits, prompt-overflow guardrail bypasses — show the attack surface widening as fast as the defenses.
- **Agents move into production — and so does agent governance.** From ClickUp swapping staff for agents to IBM and ServiceNow shipping agent-control products, the operational question is shifting from "can agents work?" to "how do we govern, attribute, and audit them?" (echoed in research on accountability boundaries and poisoned-memory auditing).
- **Frontier capability milestones keep landing.** DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus solved long-open Erdős problems for a few hundred dollars, and a coding agent autonomously discovered a compute-cutting scaling algorithm — even as Hotz, LeCun, and others caution about reliability and over-claiming.
- **The compute and hardware substrate is contested.** Memory now dominates AI-chip cost, Huawei touts a design breakthrough under sanctions, Taiwan cracks down on NVIDIA chip smuggling, and data-center demand drives fresh Japanese infrastructure spending.
- **AI ethics and trust reach the cultural mainstream.** Pope Leo XIV's encyclical, debates over AI watermarking, and research on hallucination, miscitation, and statutory staleness all converge on a single concern: whether AI outputs can be trusted and held accountable.
