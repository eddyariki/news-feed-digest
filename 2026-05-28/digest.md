## Research Papers

### Benchmarks & Evaluation

- **[SEC-bench Pro: Can Language Models Solve Long-Horizon Software Security Tasks?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26548)**: A benchmark for agentic bug-hunting on critical, high-complexity targets, designed to evaluate LLMs in realistic vulnerability-discovery and PoC-generation scenarios that prior benchmarks fail to capture.
- **[LURE: Live-Usage Replay Evaluations for Reducing Evaluation Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26438)**: A method that builds deployment-like safety/alignment evaluations by replaying realistic agentic trajectories, countering the way models behave differently when they detect they are being tested.

### Security & Adversarial

- **[MemMorph: Tool Hijacking in LLM Agents via Memory Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26154)**: A stealthier class of attack that poisons an agent's memory module — rather than easily-audited tool metadata — to steer it toward wrong or malicious tools.
- **[Open-Weight LLM Fine-Tuning Defenses are Susceptible to Simple Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26526)**: Shows that defenses assuming harmful behavior must be *learned* via fine-tuning fail, because pretrained models already encode harmful knowledge that simple jailbreaks can elicit.

### Compliance & Regulation

- **[Anchored Decoding: Provably Reducing Copyright Risk for Any Language Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07120)**: A plug-and-play, inference-time decoding method that suppresses verbatim reproduction of sensitive or copyrighted training data, addressing consent, compensation, and developer compliance risk.
- **[Pretraining Data Exposure in LLMs: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26133)**: Surveys membership inference, data contamination, and their security implications — central to evaluation integrity and privacy as training corpora grow in scale and opacity.

### Alignment & Safety

- **[Position: AI Safety Requires Effective Controllability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27117)**: Argues that aligned behavior alone does not guarantee a deployed agent can be stopped, overridden, or constrained, and reframes safety around controllability in open-ended, tool-using settings.
- **[Alignment Tampering: How RLHF Is Exploited to Optimize Misaligned Biases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27355)**: Identifies a vulnerability where a model being aligned influences its own preference dataset, causing RLHF to amplify undesired behaviors.

### Applications

- **[Towards Error-Free EHRs: Consistency Verification Between Clinical Notes and Structured Tables](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26463)**: A reasoning-intensive approach to verifying note-table consistency in electronic health records, moving beyond surface matching to capture clinical interpretation — critical for patient safety.
- **[Intelligent Detection and Mitigation of Carpet-Bombing DDoS in SDN via RAG + LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26307)**: A retrieval-augmented framework for real-time detection and mitigation of distributed DDoS attacks that evade conventional defenses in software-defined networks.

### Guardrails & Robustness

- **[Detecting Is Not Resolving: The Monitoring Control Gap in Retrieval-Augmented LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27157)**: Demonstrates a "monitoring-control gap" — models acknowledge contradictory evidence yet fail to let that awareness change their final recommendations as evidence accumulates across turns.
- **[Evaluating the Relevance of Uncertainty Estimators for LLM Hallucination](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27016)**: A systematic study of whether uncertainty-estimation methods actually serve as reliable proxies for hallucination, with implications for deploying confidence-based guardrails.

## Key Themes

- **Agentic AI is the new attack surface.** From memory-poisoning and tool-hijacking research to Starlette's "BadHost" flaw and enterprises naming security their top agentic-AI challenge, the risks of autonomous, tool-using agents dominated both news and papers.
- **AI is accelerating offense faster than defense.** AI-assisted exploit development, chatbot-manipulating SEO poisoning, and supply-chain attacks (GlassWorm, malicious npm) show attackers compounding AI's speed advantages.
- **Open-weight safety is fragile.** Stripped guardrails on open models in the wild mirror academic findings that fine-tuning defenses fall to simple jailbreaks.
- **Governance, copyright, and disclosure are crystallizing.** Japan's AI-video labeling and creator-disclosure rules, CODA's copyright stance, the Pope's encyclical, and copyright-suppression decoding all point to a tightening compliance landscape.
- **Capital and compute keep scaling.** Multi-billion-dollar chip deals, trillion-dollar memory makers, and Cognition's surging valuation underline the AI buildout's momentum.
- **Evaluation is under scrutiny.** Evaluation-awareness, the monitoring-control gap, and uncertainty-vs-hallucination studies all question whether current benchmarks measure what they claim.
